The Right Hand Behind Your Success.

My name is Annabel, and I am an Executive and Virtual Assistant based in Lagos, Nigeria. I help executives, entrepreneurs, and businesses manage the administrative and operational responsibilities that keep their work moving forward.

My experience spans entertainment, real estate, and hospitality. Working across these environments has strengthened my ability to adapt, coordinate, communicate, and keep workflows organized.

Good support takes foresight, discretion, consistency, and attention to detail, and the ability to anticipate needs before they become challenges. Whether I am coordinating schedules, organizing documents, managing correspondence, supporting customers, or assisting with daily operations, I bring professionalism and accountability.

My goal is to be a dependable extension of the people and organizations I support, so they can work smarter and act with greater confidence.

    My approach

    Understand your priorities and how you like to work. Organize responsibilities into clear systems. Execute reliably and on time. Communicate progress so nothing is left guessing.
